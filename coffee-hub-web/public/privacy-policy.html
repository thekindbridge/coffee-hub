Coffee Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Coffee Hub ("Coffee Hub", "we", "our", or "us") provides coffee ordering, order tracking, delivery operations, and store administration services through our Android app, web app, and related backend services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what choices users have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and identity data such as phone number, Firebase user ID, role assignment, and optional profile details like name and email.
  • Order and delivery data such as cart items, order history, delivery address, delivery instructions, assigned delivery agent details, and order timestamps.
  • Location data, including approximate or precise device location when shared for checkout, routing, or live delivery tracking.
  • Device and app data such as app version, browser or webview type, operating system, network metadata, and service-generated technical identifiers used for security and reliability.
  • Support and contact data when you reach out to Coffee Hub for help or account-related requests.

2. Why we collect this information

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To verify phone numbers using OTP-based sign-in.
  • To enable menu browsing, ordering, payment-flow completion, and delivery tracking.
  • To support admin and delivery-agent operations.
  • To secure the service, reduce abuse, and enforce role-based access control.
  • To investigate issues and respond to support requests.

3. Phone number and OTP verification

Coffee Hub uses Firebase Authentication for OTP-based phone sign-in. Phone numbers and verification requests may be processed by Google and Firebase anti-abuse systems. Coffee Hub also applies rate-limiting and cooldown controls to reduce OTP spam and misuse.

4. Location data

Coffee Hub requests location access only for user-facing order and delivery features. Customer location may be used during checkout to confirm a delivery destination and support live order tracking. Delivery location may be used to show live route progress during active orders. The Android release reviewed in this repository does not require background location permission.

5. Order, profile, and role data

Coffee Hub stores operational records in Firebase Firestore, including customer profile details, saved delivery information, order records, delivery session details, and access or role records for customer, admin, and delivery workflows.

6. Third-party services and processors

Coffee Hub uses Google Firebase Authentication, Google Cloud Firestore, Google Maps or related location services where enabled, and Vercel-hosted backend infrastructure to operate the service. Coffee Hub does not sell personal data.

7. Data sharing

Coffee Hub shares data only as needed to run the service, fulfill orders, secure the platform, or comply with law. This can include sharing authentication or anti-abuse data with Google Firebase, map and routing requests with Google services, and limited operational data with admins and delivery agents when required to fulfill an order.

8. Security measures

  • Encrypted network transport through HTTPS and Firebase-managed secure connections.
  • Authentication and role-based authorization controls.
  • Firestore security rules that restrict access by user identity and role.
  • Server-side validation for sensitive order and role-management actions.
  • OTP request throttling and abuse controls.

9. Data retention

Coffee Hub retains data only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate ordering and delivery services, maintain account records and order history, investigate abuse or disputes, and comply with legal or operational obligations. Some information may be deleted sooner upon request, while some transactional or security records may be retained for a limited period where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

10. User rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access or update your account details, request deletion of your account and associated personal data, withdraw optional permissions such as location access, and contact Coffee Hub with privacy questions.

Account deletion requests can be initiated through the Coffee Hub account deletion page or by contacting support. Identity verification may be required before deletion is processed.

11. Children's privacy

Coffee Hub is intended for general food-ordering use and is not designed for children under 13.
