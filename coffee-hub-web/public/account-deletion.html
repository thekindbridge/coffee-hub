Coffee Hub Account Deletion Request

Last updated: May 6, 2026

If you want Coffee Hub to delete your app account and associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request using the support contact below.

How to request deletion

  1. Email Coffee Hub support from an address you can monitor.
  2. Include the phone number registered with your Coffee Hub account.
  3. If you have one, include a recent order ID to help us verify the request.
  4. State that you want your Coffee Hub account deleted.
Email deletion request

What happens next

  • Coffee Hub may request limited information to verify your identity before deletion.
  • Your account access and related personal data will be deleted or anonymized where feasible.
  • Some operational, fraud-prevention, or legally required records may be retained for a limited period.
