## Full description

Coffee Hub makes it easy to order your favorite coffee, tea, snacks, and fast bites from one smooth mobile experience.

Browse the latest menu, add items to your cart, confirm your delivery location, and place orders in seconds. Once your order is live, Coffee Hub helps you stay updated with real-time order progress and delivery tracking so you know exactly what is happening next.

Coffee Hub is built for more than checkout. Behind the scenes, the same platform supports store operations with admin controls for menu updates, order handling, timing control, and delivery coordination. That means customers, store admins, and delivery staff all work from one connected system designed for speed and accuracy.

Why Coffee Hub stands out:

- quick OTP-based sign-in with secure Firebase authentication;
- easy coffee and snack ordering from a mobile-friendly menu;
- fast checkout with delivery address and location support;
- real-time order status and live delivery visibility;
- connected admin and delivery workflows for smoother fulfillment.

Whether you are ordering your daily coffee, tracking a delivery, or managing store operations, Coffee Hub keeps the experience simple, reliable, and fast.
